# Botium Toys Security Audit

A simulated internal security audit completed as part of the
Google Cybersecurity Certificate.

## Overview

I reviewed the provided scope, goals, and risk assessment for
Botium Toys, a fictional company, and evaluated its existing
security controls and compliance practices.

## My Work

- Completed the controls and compliance checklist.
- Identified gaps in access control, encryption, password
  management, detection, and recovery.
- Developed recommendations based on the documented risks.
- Proposed ownership, deadlines, and verification measures
  for remediation tasks.

## Key Findings

- Employees had excessive access to sensitive customer data.
- Customer payment information lacked encryption.
- Critical data had no backups or disaster recovery plan.
- An intrusion detection system was missing.
- Asset inventory and classification needed improvement.

## Recommendations

Prioritize least privilege, encryption, protected and tested
backups, and disaster recovery planning. Strengthen password
management, implement intrusion detection, and establish
regular asset and legacy system reviews.

## Reflection

This was my first security audit. I learned to distinguish
least privilege from separation of duties, separate detection
from recovery, and connect recommendations to documented
evidence.

## Scope and Attribution

The scenario and assessment templates were provided through
the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. My work consisted of
the completed assessment and recommendations.

This was a document-based educational exercise. I did not
test live systems or implement the recommended controls.
